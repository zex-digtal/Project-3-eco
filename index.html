Our Story

Growth and the Planet, Side by Side

We started Zex Digital in 2021 with one belief — that digital growth and environmental responsibility are not opposites. Most agencies treat sustainability like a marketing pillar. We treat it like a product feature.

From day one, every campaign we launch is offset, every site we ship is hosted on renewables, and every report we send shows you both your revenue and your carbon impact in the same view. Today, 200+ Indian brands trust us to grow them — and to do it cleanly.
